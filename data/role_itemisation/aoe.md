# AoE
- Best items: Blue Monolith, Stealth Dagger, Dissonance

## Explanation
The AoE category struggles a little to differentiate itself, and instead slots somewhere in between the Assassin and Multi-Hitter archetypes. While not very similar in mechanical terms to Assassins, your approach is similar - deal as much damage before you die as possible; mechanically they're much closer to Multi-Hitters, the main difference being that multi-hitters tend to have lower damage on each target, while AoE heroes deal full damage to everything (but usually with a drawback of some kind).

There aren't many true AoE heroes in the game - in fact, that list above is arguably all of them - and they basically all use magic items. Look to maximise your damage potential and don't worry too much about anything else - damage, damage and more damage. Debuffs and such are nice, but they can be applied by heroes with less power behind them. Your AoE-users are there to throw the biggest, most potent splashes of damage possible before they inevitably get shanked.

## Example Builds
- Blue Monolith, Locust Swarm, Archmage Hat
- Psychic Glove, Stealth Dagger, Locust Swarm
