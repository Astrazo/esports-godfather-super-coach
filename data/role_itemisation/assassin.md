# Assassin
- Best items: Little Lightning, Conversion Gear, Obedient Whip, Sharp Piercer

## Explanation
Quite the contrast to the previous archetypes, Assassin itemisation is all damage, all the time. Disregard any downsides, you don't care how squishy you get - that ADC can't hurt you if he's dead before he gets to shoot, after all.

Notably, there are two kinds of items that can reasonably fall into the Assassin category. The first is the more obvious Assassin item - things with a whole lot of damage and no defences, or sometimes even negative defences. The second kind, on the other hand, is less obvious - offensive items with smaller bonuses whose main benefit is they're cheap. Assassins like to snowball off kills, and one way to do that is to make your items cheap enough to chain together, letting you turn one kill into many more.

Much like with bruisers, you should usually avoid crit items for your Assassins - you want to reliably murder squishies, not do it if you land your 40% crit. The obvious exception to this rule is when your hero's mechanics revolve around crits such as with Manta or Lan - for the former you want enough crit to hit 100% without going over, and for the latter you want as much as you can get.

Note that as with ADCs, there's an argument to be had for picking up a GA effect like Phoenix Feather or Dissonance on your Assassins. In this case, though, it's usually not worth it - Assassins are usually melee and you expect them to fall over in teamfights. You just need to build to make sure they deal crippling damage before they do.

## Example Builds
- Little Lightning, Sharp Piercer, Carnivore's Medical Record
- Handy Knife, Isabelle, Obedient Whip
