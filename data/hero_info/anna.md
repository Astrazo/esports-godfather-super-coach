# Hero Summary
Support with a light card footprint who provides support for, and protection from, skirmish comps.

# Hero Analysis
## Hero Description
Anna has a lot of disparate effects between her passives and cards, but they're all fairly light touch. Her most ubiquitous trait is Fragrance - whenever your team enters a fight they gain Fragrance, which increases their armour by 40 for the first round of the fight (even if she's dead or absent). While 40 armour isn't exactly gamebreaking, it's certainly enough to make a big difference in damage output during that first round, and can really cause problems for teams that want to fight you constantly.

On top of this, Anna also provides Trial Before Awarding (activates whether she's there or not, but does not activate if she's dead). This allows you to pick a specific enemy hero to debuff (though the debuff is just a marker with no negative effects). If that hero dies during the fight, their killer is awarded with Rosemary, a buff which lasts for the rest of the game and gives that hero a 30/42 AP boost on their first attack of each fight.

Between these two effects, Anna is obviously built for frequent fights - if you're skirmishing you'll get steadily increasing benefits from securing kills while also being relatively resilient to the counterpunch. Unfortunately Rosemary tends not to be all that impactful, as it's low impact (30/42 AP isn't all that much really unless you've got a big multiplier behind it, plus it's only for the one attack) and also really quite hard to manipulate onto the hero you want getting it. Consider Anna a Fragrance vector, with Rosemary being a small added benefit, rather than the inverse.

Finally, Anna's cards are quite unique in that she actually only has one card that starts in the deck. When you play that one card you generate three more cards, all of which single-use, in your deck. This keeps your deck nice and lean, especially if you're picking her to ward off skirmishes and therefore don't want to use her cards much (which are largely skirmish-centric).

Between her light touch and her low impact but ubiquitous and positive effects, Anna comes in as a nice flexible defensive support you can drop into most comps without too many concerns. Her only real weakness (such as it is) is being drafted against teamfight comps, as her effects all wear off quickly and have no real benefit in longer, larger engages.

# Cards
## Card 1
### Description
Spawn one copy each of Mandala Flower, Flame Orchid and Cosmos into your deck.

### Card Analysis
Quite expensive for not actually doing anything directly, by playing this card you're basically pre-paying SP for the high efficiency of the created cards. The cost reduction mastery helps to mitigate the delayed impact.

## Card 2
### Description
Target an enemy hero (global); start a standard 3/1 fight centred on their hex.

### Card Analysis
A vanilla initiation card but without range restrictions and with a steep cost reduction. Most of the SP you paid for Floral Language is going into the cost reduction for this card. Nothing particularly stand-out about it otherwise aside from working on any lane.

## Card 3
### Description
Target a friendly hero. If that hero has jungle creeps within 1 hex of them, they kill all of said creeps. If they don't, they gain the Flame Orchid effect, giving them 15 armour penetration on their first attack of their next fight.

### Card Analysis
The least useful of the three spawned cards, Flame Orchid is mostly useful for situational minor counterjungling; while this doesn't tend to matter all that much in the short term, the card is at least free (once created, that is). Try to avoid playing this with no creeps around, as the Flame Orchid effect is really quite unimpressive - like Rosemary it only works for one attack, but unlike Rosemary it only lasts for one fight. Can be situationally strong for a small number of heroes that hit very hard in the first round (e.g. Hakuna).

## Card 4
### Description
Target a friendly hero and move them one hex.

### Card Analysis
Very simple and somewhat low impact, but happily does at least give you a movement effect that doesn't break the bank. Works nicely paired with Mandala Flower to give you a properly set up skirmish for very low relative SP cost, allowing you to fit in utility or pre-fight poke.

# Item Build
Support. Note that Fragrance counts as Anna taking an action and applying a buff, so any effects which proc off buffs (e.g. Scallion Oil Noodles) work nicely.

# Funnelling
No. She has no effects to make her worth stat-boosting, and tutoring her isn't really worthwhile.
