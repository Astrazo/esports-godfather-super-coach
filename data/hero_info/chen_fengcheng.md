# Hero Summary
Bruiser that shows up with a big hit and then does little else.

# Hero Analysis
## Attributes
### Hero Class
- Fighter
### Attack Type
- Melee
### Damage Type
- Physical

## Hero Description
Chen Fengcheng (hereafter referred to as Chen because I'm lazy) has two mechanics in his toolbox, both of which revolve around Ship Coins. He generates 20 Ship Coins per turn on the field, plus an extra 4, 3 and 3 from masteries (total of 30 for those of you that are REALLY bad at maths).

These Ship Coins have two uses. Their main use is on entering objective fights (or any fight after use of Expedition Preparation). In these circumstances Chen will start the round by crashing an inexplicably ghostly ship into the enemy team; total damage is equal to his Ship Coins plus 100/135 per level. This damage is applied evenly across the team (before mitigation). The second, much less interesting use is through Divide the Spoils, which splits half his Ship Coins as gold between all living heroes.

The main point of interest for Chen is that his special attack's damage is divided between enemies. The actual base damage is pretty high - he can easily be putting out ~2000 damage in early fights - but when split across a lot of enemies it becomes just a gentle tap. To really see value from it you're going to need small fights, at most two enemies (preferably one); anything more and it'll water down too much. The trouble with that, however, is that his special attack is all he's got; once it's spent he's left with wet noodle slaps for attacks and nothing else. This leaves you in the awkward position of either needing to be targeting a single very squishy opponent, or having a pocket jungler around to deal the actual damage.

This is further exacerbated by the fact that you're not getting the special attack at all (outside objective fights) unless you play the painfully expensive Expedition Preparation first, and that gets eaten from a single fight unless that fight is coming from Chen's own initiation card. So not only do you need very specific fight participation, you also need a very specific card to be initiating with. And on top of that, if the enemy team initiate a fight with him, say goodbye to your boat.

All of this could be taken to mean he's a bad hero. He's actually not - he just needs to specifically sit in top lane, where he'll rarely be ganked and he's got a reasonable shot at pulling in a 1v1 or 2v1 fight with Burn, Kill and Loot. You'll hopefully only need to drop the 5 SP once or twice in the game, and can smash the (presumably squishy) opposing laner to pieces with your weaponised maritime vessel. A very specific pick, then - but an effective one.

# Cards
## Expedition Preparation
### Description
When Chen next participates in a fight, he may use his special attack. This effect is not expended when participating in objective fights, the Final Fight, or if initiated by Burn, Kill and Loot.

### Card Analysis
This is essentially your tax for playing Chen. It does absolutely nothing on its own, so you're paying 5 SP so that he actually does something when he next fights. For obvious reasons, you reeeeeally want to avoid situations that will make you pay that 5 SP again.

## Burn, Kill, and Loot
### Description
Initiate a fight on an enemy hero within 2 hexes. 2 hex radius, 3 round duration. If you kill something, search Divide the Spoils after the fight.

### Card Analysis
And this is how you get to use the boat without paying more taxes (who'd have thought a pirate would be all about tax evasion). A decent range, 2 hex-radius initiation would be a good card on most heroes, but for Chen that radius is a severe detriment - the last thing he wants is other people soaking some of the blast. This card is a great argument for passing Deployment Phases with little to no SP expenditure repeatedly, waiting for the perfect opportunity - you want your jungler to path near top while the enemy jungler is bot side. Incidentally, for those of you considering a little pirating yourself, this would be a terrible order to do things in - you'll only be looting ashes. Instead, kill first, loot, then burn last.

## Reward and Punish
### Description
Attack an enemy hero within 1 hex. Attack always hits, and Chen gains gold and Ship Coins equal to the damage dealt.

### Card Analysis
Doesn't look impressive on the surface - just Hass' Shadow Blade but paying 1 less SP for half as much gold. The real value is in the Ship Coins though - your one attack's worth of damage is converted into Coins which will then apply that damage to every boat-bomb for the rest of the game. If you're playing to pick up isolated top lane kills (and you should be, if you're picking Chen) then that's definitely worth the SP cost.

## Divide the Spoils
### Description
All living heroes (friend or foe) gain gold; the amount is equal to 50% of Chen's Ship Coins divided equally among them.

### Card Analysis
The much less impressive payoff for your Ship Coins. Since the gold is divided between both teams, you're specifically looking to play this when you have more living heroes than the opposing team does; however, if only one enemy is dead (i.e. a 5-4 split) you're only getting a tiny relative gold gain (1/18th of Chen's Ship Coins is the delta). It's a benefit, sure, but a tiny one. There are two ways to use this, therefore - you can either avoid playing it at all unless the stars align and you get a great fight result, or (if you're smart) you can tailor your item builds towards more expensive items and play this whenever you have it. If both teams get extra gold, the ones with the more expensive items benefit more. Alternatively, just hit tab in every DP and see if you'll hit an item spike by playing this. Note that while it's not made very clear, these are one-time-use - you get one for each equipment you activate. That's arguably a good thing, because it protects your deck from this mediocrity.

# Variants
None.

# Item Build
One slap chap. He gets his one big attack off (you hope), then down to lacklustre bruiser stats for the rest of the fight. You can itemise him with maximised AP - that will give him extra damage for Reward and Punish and also the damage to follow up in his solo fights - but expect him to severely underperform in teamfights if you do.

# Funnelling
Tutoring is decent, since you'll want to Burn, Kill and Loot whenever you have a good opening to justify the prep cost. Stat gains aren't, really, since there's no AP scaling on the special attack - you'll get some benefit through increased Reward and Punish damage.

# Interactions
Damn you, Anna - Turns out when literally all your power is in the first round, you get screwed if the enemy team have a lot of armour that round. Arguably a hard counter.

Not Charming - You'd think Divide the Spoils would pair nicely with Gillis' Charm to generate bonus gold. Unfortunately, it really isn't - the actual gold gain is minimal, so paying an extra 4 SP to replicate it really doesn't do much. If you're running an Acedia already, on the other hand, it's a little extra on the side I suppose.

Centaurs are bad sailors - Unfortunately Reinhardt is not the great synergy you'd hope for with Chen thanks to the foibles of action order. Chen will smash his ship to splinters for the eighth time that game, then Reinhardt will shred the enemies' armour afterwards. They really need to have a chat with each other.
