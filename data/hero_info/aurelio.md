# Hero Summary
Strong all-rounder Fighter, stacks up a huge amount of crit and crit damage.

# Hero Analysis
## Hero Description
Aurelio is a solid generic Fighter choice. He's pretty decently tanky, but more importantly, can build up to deal a substantial amount of damage thanks to his Flame mechanic. It's important to note that the Flame buff can stack up as much as you like, but he can only gain one stack per enemy hero in fights - that means that if you want him to go off in a big way, you're going to need to play Protagonist as many times as possible prior to an important fight.

Regardless of how many stacks he can get, though, it's entirely possible for Aurelio to get stuck on a single target, often a tank, and wind up being extremely underwhelming in fights; if he can't get attacks on multiple targets to reach high crit levels and a high crit damage multiplier, he's not going to perform. This means that generally you don't want to prioritise him against balanced enemy teams with both solid frontline and backline. Instead, pick him into teams with a lot of frontline Fighters - enough melees that he'll be happy enough spreading his attacks around, none of which are building heavy defences. Give him a decent team comp to work against and some backup on the frontline and he'll be your quintessential Fighter - dealing enough damage to kill things while remaining tanky enough to eat a few hits without falling over.

There are two reasons to pick Aurelio in your comp. The first is as a safe blind pick (at least if Hass is banned out) - he may not excel, but he can perform just fine without support in most comps. The second, much more notable, reason is to lean hard on bot lane. Your one initiation is too expensive to play without a lot of targets, but between Inferno March and Flame Assault you've got enough mobility to cross the length of the map and slap those squishies in the head. Gank bot early and often, snowball Aurelio, be happy.

Note that while you can pick Mastery choices which make him into a passable damage sponge, it's really not worth it - Aurelio's mechanics scream 'damage', Flame stacks come in over time, and he has no other utility. Ask yourself: do you want a fairly tanky high damage bruiser, or do you want a not-actually-very-tanky tank?

# Cards
## Inferno March
### Description
Move up to 2 hexes, then attack all enemies and monsters within 1 hex. Gain 1 SP for each target hit.

### Card Analysis
This card does a lot of heavy lifting for Aurelio, acting as a combination movement card, SP generation and light poke. Realistically though, its main use is as SP generation - anything you get out of it beyond that is a bonus. You'll usually want to target the hex you'll get the most mileage out of it therefore, ensuring there's at least three monsters in range so you'll refund your expenditure as a minimum - and if they're coming from the enemy jungle, all the better.

## Protagonist
### Description
Gain 2 stacks of Flame, then search Flame Assault into your hand.

### Card Analysis
A very useful card, particularly with the Mastery choice to give you more Flame stacks. Going into a fight already stacked up is extremely valuable, and given that the card costs nothing you'd be a fool not to play it on cooldown.

## Flame Assult
### Description
Start a fight with an enemy hero within 2 hexes and all heroes within 1 hex of them. Duration is equal to number of participants.

### Card Analysis
An initiation card pretty much custom-built for ganking bot. The prohibitive cost makes playing it on any other lane unappealing - you'd be paying 8 SP for a vanilla Initiation card with extra range - but jumping over to bot for a nice five round beating is great. Even so, given the cost you're probably not going to play this without discounts or Inferno March.

# Item Build
Bruiser. You may want to sprinkle in some crit or crit damage, but you probably don't want to go full glass unless you've got tank(s) and they're getting early Presence. The exception is if you're on Variant 1 (Flame II giving lower crit chance but higher crit damage) - that Variant pushes you towards itemising crit so you get to throw the crazy 300% crit damage hits around more often.

# Funnelling
One of the better Bruisers to funnel, both for cards and stats. Innate crit while being a frontline bruiser lets him use pretty much any stats you can give him, and searching his cards lets him doublejump to bot lane freely. Still probably suboptimal since his teamfight performance is unreliable, but you could definitely do a lot worse.
