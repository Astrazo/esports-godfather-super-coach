# Hero Summary
Vanilla ADC with a powerful but fragile summon.

# Hero Analysis
## Attributes
### Hero Class
- Marksman
### Attack Type
- Ranged
### Damage Type
- Physical

## Hero Description
Omaha is what happens when you take Kaka and make him not terrible. In fights he summons Wild Heart, a summon which has the same AP as Omaha (if you're building right) and 25% of Omaha's max health (Plus some more - bonus health should be 50x Omaha's level with masteries but never seems to quite line up). Wild Heart also shreds armour equal to Omaha's level plus 5 with each attack, and unlike Kaka's Stone Hound, doesn't cause Omaha to spontaneously explode when killed. Oh, and you can send Wild Heart off to help someone else and he'll still come be present if Omaha fights in the meantime. Really, everything Kaka does wrong, Omaha does right.

This leaves Omaha in a position of dealing extremely high potential damage; he gets his own vanilla ADC attacks, plus a summon with all of his AP and a sizeable armour shred on the back of it. The downside is that Wild Heart is extremely squishy due to its very small health pool and being melee; while it costs you nothing in the longer run to have him die, once he goes down in a fight you're left with an entirely ability-less ADC. Note also that while Wild Heart inherits Omaha's AP, it doesn't receive crit chance, crit damage, or any of his item effects (much like a Zealot clone).

Omaha winds up falling into a slot in the middle of the tiers for ADCs. He has aforementioned great damage output, but Wild Heart is too squishy to be reliable; while his damage is technically higher than someone like Elemi, she at least keeps putting her damage out until she goes down. As soon as Wild Heart dies, Omaha's actual damage output isn't high enough to compare to any other ADC. You should only pick Omaha, therefore, if you've had a strangely high number of ADC bans come through - or if you're trying to run a very skirmish-heavy playstyle, which his cards do well.

# Cards
## Sentinel
### Description
Choose an allied hero (global). For the next 5 rounds, if that hero fights, Wild Heart also joins the fight.

### Card Analysis
A nice way to get an advantage in skirmishes anywhere on the map for the cost of a hand slot. Surprisingly impactful due to Wild Heart's high AP total; can also be used to ward off ganks thanks to the birb soaking a couple of hits. Doesn't stop Omaha getting his birb when he fights, and also works for objective fights (provided you pick someone who's going to be in that fight).

## Eagle Soar
### Description
Choose an enemy hero (global); start a fight with all heroes within 1 hex of the target (3 round duration). Wild Heart joins this fight.

### Card Analysis
A pretty good initiation card - it's global, you get Wild Heart on your side, and it costs less than a vanilla initiation card to boot (with masteries, that is). Note that since you get Wild Heart either way, there's no need to wait for Sentinel to play this.

# Variants

## Variant 1

### Gained
- Nothing

### Removed
- Nothing

### Changed
- Wild Heart's on-hit armour interaction changed from shredding armour to ignoring armour.
- Armour affected by Wild Heart increased from 1 to 2 times Omaha's level.

### Variant Description
An overall nerf - while this does boost the birb's damage in smaller engagements, this comes at the cost of the armour shred, which would boost everyone else's damage as well. What's worse, the armour shred sticks around for the entire fight, while Wild Heart is probably falling over in the first round. Omaha was already pretty mid in vanilla, you don't want to draft him when this Variant is knocking around; you could technically draft some heavy Presence tanks and/or Peter to keep the birb around longer, but at that point you're throwing half your draft into protecting a summon which is not all that wise.

# Item Build
ADC. Don't be tempted to build flat AP to help scale Wild Heart - with that health total he's not making it to the end of the fight (he's lucky to see round 2).

# Funnelling
Not really worth, at least any more than any other ADC. Bonus AP might help Wild Heart a little, but as noted, he's falling over fast no matter what you do; bonus health on Omaha does nothing for the birb. Can be worth tutoring if you want to take advantage of the cheaper, global Initiation with attached poultry.

# Interactions
Vulnerable Birb - Omaha becomes trash tier if Wild Heart goes down early; as such, he's vulnerable to the various ways in which that can happen. Substantial AoE from Niels or Enidi, assassination from Xiangxi Ke, even Nihil's lower damage numbers are enough to put Wild Heart down in a couple of rounds. Foso, of course, will simply delete it.

Killdeer with the broken wing - Omaha's low health total can work in your favour if you're facing low-health assassination heroes, since he's almost always going to come in with a lower health total than your actual heroes. This lets you soak murderous backstabs with a disposable birb. Remember Xiangxi Ke gets a free attack when an enemy dies, though, so it's not all that effective at warding her off.

Kid - Minor point, but despite not being a hero Wild Heart counts as a target for the purposes of Kid's stat boosts. Omaha can slot nicely into a Kid-centric comp so that he'll get a nice chunk of AP when Wild Heart is inevitably sniped. v3.1 addition Fernan also gets his own version of this effect, and so can get some nice benefits picked into an Omaha.
