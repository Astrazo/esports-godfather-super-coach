# Hero Summary
True damage critting melee who primarily hunts isolated tanks.

# Hero Analysis
## Attributes
### Hero Class
- Gladiator
### Attack Type
- Melee
### Damage Type
- Physical

## Hero Description
Manta rejoices in the unique effect that makes his crits deal true damage, bypassing all damage reduction effects and even ignoring immunity to crits (Bart cowers in fear). He also gains 25% crit rate innately, with another 10% from masteries and increased by another 25% if there's only one enemy hero in the fight.

All of this puts Manta in a slightly awkward position, itemisation-wise. He wants to primarily be hunting isolated targets (as Undercurrent can't work unless the target is isolated and he gains the crit bonus) and wants to build at least a decent amount of crit, which pushes him towards the Assassin route; meanwhile, the fact that his crits inflict true damage makes him want to be hitting tanks, which inclines him to more of a Fighter build.

The general use-case for Manta is to take him mid into a squishy mid-laner you want to lean on to weaken for teamfights; he's a great pick into something like a Cubey or Dylan mid lane to keep them out of the way and their cards out of hand. He can also play into lane bullies like Qube and Justice since his isolation crit gives him a major leg-up in the 1v1.

You can take Manta in the jungle too, but this is more of a niche situation because he struggles with bot lane ganks (which are a jungler's bread-and-butter). In this case he's more suited for leaning into top lane ganks, using his crit boost to bring down bruisers and tanks in the 2v1. It's unusual that you'll have a top laner that needs shutting down like this, but if the AI decides to draft a top lane Cubey or swaps Felyn up there into a tank it may be necessary.

# Cards
## Undercurrent
### Description
While not in your hand, gains 1 token per round. When played, targets an enemy within 2 hexes that has no nearby allies and starts a fight, during which Manta gains 4% bonus AP for each token.

### Card Analysis
Very much an assassination initiation rather than a normal one due to the single target restriction; this makes for quite an awkward-to-use card since frequently your desired target won't be available through simple pathing. What's more, the token system it uses ensures that you won't get as much benefit from spamming it, reducing its potential even more. Its main purpose is to give you a cheap way of starting a 1v1 in lane, along with the bonus AP to win it.

## Trident of Radiance
### Description
Choose a direction and attack all monsters, enemy heroes and towers in that direction, dealing 2 damage to towers and causing pushback on heroes struck.

### Card Analysis
Very much designed for laning, you need the stars to align nicely for you to get a good line-up from the jungle (or to manipulate your SP by taking short turns, at any rate). From the jungle this is best used for counterjungling; if Manta's ganking is going to be restricted by Undercurrent's awkwardness, you may as well hold back the enemy jungler to stop them scaling as well. Note that if your Manta happens to push under the mid tower you can double dip, which is nice.

## Wave Assult
### Description
Target an enemy hero within 2 hexes. Manta and any allies within 1 hex of the target attack it.

### Card Analysis
Expensive for what it does, which is generally get a maximum of 3 attacks off (and frequently less than that). Once again an awkward card, you'll usually want to play this in bot lane to get your money's worth, but bot lane is the lane Manta usually can't gank. The main use for this, rather than the attacks themselves, is to stack more tokens on Undercurrent (via masteries) to allow for a more effective solo kill, and it's absurdly expensive for that purpose when the entire point of Undercurrent is to be cheap.

# Variants

## Variant 1

### Gained
- Manta gains 10% crit chance on killing an enemy hero, to a maximum of 30%.

### Removed
- Manta gains 25% crit chance when only one enemy unit remains.

### Changed
- Nothing

### Variant Description
This Variant makes Manta much stronger - that bonus crit is permanent and goes a long way towards helping him attain his goal of 100% crit. It is of course dependent on Manta getting the kills, which can be awkward when teammates finish off wounded enemies... but fortunately you have an initiation card specifically designed for 1v1s against isolated enemies. Moves Manta up the tier list by almost a full tier since he can now treat teamfights like 1v1s and act like a scarier Aurelio in general.

## Variant 2

### Gained
- After each attack Manta loses 15% crit chance for the rest of the fight, stacking up to three times.

### Removed
- Manta gains 25% crit chance when only one enemy unit remains.

### Changed
- Manta's increased crit chance increased from 25% to 50%.

### Variant Description
A major downgrade. Hypothetically makes Manta better at early game ganking from the jungle, but in practice means he gets a tiny bit better at the bot lane ganks he's not designed for and much worse at the 1v1 kills he's supposed to be getting. Don't pick Manta with this Variant active.

# Item Build
Assassin. You want to be bringing Manta to murder mid lane over and over (I now realise), so you want to itemise for that. Build exactly enough crit to get to 100% on isolated targets (so 40% or 50% depending on mastery level) then AP after that. Remember his crits deal true damage, so armour pen is pointless.

# Funnelling
Probably not. Undercurrent is designed to deter you from playing it too often, and the rest of his cards are kinda garbage. Meanwhile, weakened performance in teamfights makes stat investment less efficient, though you may rethink this if Variant 1 is in effect.

# Interactions
Dylan - While very few heroes have any particular synergy with Manta, Dylan's unique ability to grant crit chance and damage to an ally via Strengthening Potion can help tremendously to give Manta reliability.

Bart - What beats crit immunity? Crit immunity immunity!

Anna - Cheap, brutal 1v1 kills are Manta's byword, and Anna is a great support for aiding skirmishes. Her free armour will make sure you come out on top of your early 1v1s, and the bonus AP from Rosemary will then help secure later ones - to the point of being able to use Mandala Flower as a bootleg Undercurrent. Alternatively, Mandala Flower also lets a jungle Manta grab a gank in bot lane without having to draft in Reserve Tactics.
