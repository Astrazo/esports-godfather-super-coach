# Hero Summary
Unimpressive alpha strike Assassin/Fighter.

# Hero Analysis
## Attributes
### Hero Class
- Assassin
### Attack Type
- Melee
### Damage Type
- Physical

## Hero Description
Lubos is an extremely underwhelming melee who can be built as either an Assassin or Fighter. His entire kit is invested in a single ability - when entering a fight, he replaces his first attack (doesn't get a standard attack in the first round, unlike Reinhardt) with a targeted strike which deals (with masteries) 160% normal damage, ignores 30 armour, and shreds 25 armour from the target.

This single attack is very strong in and of itself, primarily because the armour shred lasts for the duration of the entire fight; unfortunately, it's literally everything Lubos brings to the table. He has no other effects, so once the first round has passed he's purely vanilla. This means that while the game tags him as an Assassin, he's actually better off being built as a Fighter unless you're basing your strategy around lots of small skirmishes - in larger engagements, you're better off building him to soak a few hits rather than trying to build entirely around a single attack.

Lubos' only other claim to fame is having the only example in the entire game of an initiation card with no round limit on it. These tools aren't enough to make him worth picking, unfortunately (it's of limited use starting a fight with no round limit when it's a fight you're unlikely to win from a level playing field), so Lubos should generally be avoided.

Lubos has, at least, gained a solid synergistic pick in DLC 2 hero Leon. Both are able to target a specific enemy hero in fights; Leon's weakness is his low damage output, while Lubos' is that he hits reasonably hard, but only once before losing his targeting. The two working together are able to single out and kill a specific squishy target quickly, at least so long as Brand stacks match up, and while this isn't enough to make Lubos a good pick it does at least create niche comp potential.

# Cards
## Knight's Oath
### Description
Target an ally. For six rounds, if the targeted ally fights, Lubos will fight whether he's in range or not. Doesn't apply to special teamfights.

### Card Analysis
Really not a good card. It is at least global, but that's really not enough to justify dropping 2 SP ensuring one of the weaker heroes in the game participates in a fight; 2 SP is much, if not all, of your pre-initiation budget. You can also use this pre-emptively to head off a gank you can see coming, but even then this is still pretty meh.

## Duel
### Description
Initiate a fight with an enemy within 1 hex and all other heroes within 1 hex. Lasts until Lubos or his target dies.

### Card Analysis
Unique, as the only initiation card in the game (besides objective-based initiation cards) with no round limitation. Other than that it's pretty generic as a one hex, 6 SP initiation; the guarantee of a kill happening at some point is nice, but has a nasty tendency to mean missed kills when your target dies prematurely.

# Variants

## Variant 1

### Gained
- Lubos' special attack always crits.

### Removed
- Lubos' special attack deals 135% damage and removes 15 armour.

### Changed
- Nothing

### Variant Description
Depending on itemisation this can often wind up being a substantial nerf - base crit damage is only 150%, and the armour shred is useful for follow-up. If your current version has some nice crit damage boosting gear, though, you can build Lubos up to be a nasty instakill assassin, especially if you can lean on a lane to keep one target weak. Very snowbally.

## Variant 2

### Gained
- Nothing

### Removed
- Nothing

### Changed
- Lubos' special-attack damage reduced from 135% to 75%.
- Armour removed by Lubos' special attack increased from 15 to 25.

### Variant Description
Generally a buff as well, despite the sizeable damage reduction - under normal circumstances Lubos isn't going to be one-shotting anything and so will always be relying on follow-up from other heroes. Shredding an extra 10 armour will usually make up the damage difference inside 1-2 attacks, so you're much more likely to see that targeted enemy fall over by the end of the fight. Also helps Lubos win Duels against health-stacking enemies like Frank.

# Item Build
Bruiser / Assassin. You'll only want to build him Assassin under specific circumstances, those being when version/itemisation strongly encourages it or when you've got a comp built around exploding one target immediately. Otherwise just take your first turn armour shred and make the best of it.

# Funnelling
No. Only two cards, one of which is barely worth playing. Pouring stats into a one pump chump isn't efficient in the slightest.

# Interactions
Targeted attacks - Thanks to his special attack striking whatever you tell it to, you can sometimes deal enough damage to make a specific enemy hero the lowest health target. This lets you pick a specific target for heroes like Nihil and Xiangxi Ke to go after - and the armour shred will help them finish the job. New arrival Leon is perhaps the best choice for this, though you'll need to be careful to send Lubos after whoever has the most Brand stacks.

Lady Deadfire - Notable for giving up to 3x damage from a single attack; the combination of Lubos and Lady Deadfire can instakill a squishy target before the fight really starts. This is probably the only reason to build Lubos as an assassin rather than a Fighter.
