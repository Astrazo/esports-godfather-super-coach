# Bruiser
- Best items: Charged Barrel, Mechanical Arm, Dragon Slayer, Golden Ticket

## Explanation
At last moving away from the Pure Tank itemisation categories and into something with some weight being it, the Bruiser is a catch-all term for anything which is bulky but expected to be a durable damage-dealer rather than a bullet sponge with a fist attached. Notably the game separates this into the Fighter and Gladiator subcategories, but this only really influences base stat growth.

Usually you'll be looking for items here which boost both damage and survivability to some extent or other, or which provide a lot of flat stats without needing to synergise with others. You should avoid items which place much emphasis on crit (though don't disregard crit items entirely, some like Bunny Dagger may be worthy of consideration in a weaker item version).

IMPORTANT - Plate of Agony would be a great item for this kind of hero, but currently seems to be bugged - so far as I can tell, it functions exactly how it's supposed to except its stacks never clear. This means that in longer fights, rather than delaying incoming damage, it instead stacks up huge amounts and unavoidably kills you.
This seems to have been patched at this point, but still use at your own risk.

## Example Builds
- Chicken Rib, Dragon Slayer, Gravity Converter
- Height Detector, Golden Ticket, Mechanical Arm
