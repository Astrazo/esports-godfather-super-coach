# ADC
- Best items: Sword of Heaven, Handy Knife, Laser Sword, Boxing Gloves

## Explanation
You'll have an ADC in the vast majority of comps you play, and for good reason - you'll usually need someone whose job it is to deliver single big punches in teamfights. ADCs have very low defensive stats and there's not really any getting around that; don't worry about defensive items except maybe a GA effect (or Stealth Dagger on the ADCs that have access to it).

The big difference between ADC and Assassin itemisation is you'll care a lot less about budget for ADCs and a lot more about crit. ADCs have naturally high AP which works very nicely with crit; you'll almost always want to be aiming for as close to 100% as you can reasonably get. The only time you won't want to build crit items on your ADC is if they're slotting into another itemisation archetype (like Felyn) or there just aren't enough crit items in your version to make a build out of.

Don't worry too much about item costs for your ADC - their entire reason to exist is for insurance for late game teamfights. Don't get silly with it of course - BaJie is the only one who can pick the Ultimate Iron Fist without being comically optimistic - but expect your build cost to break 9k gold. The exception to that rule is with first-build items, like stacking ones (Physics Textbook can be very nice) or Chicken Rib, which can help to bring your overall cost down to something a little less exorbitant.

## Example Builds
- Sword of Heaven, Handheld Telescope, Laser Sword
- Physics Textbook, Limitless Chip, Carnivore's Medical Record
