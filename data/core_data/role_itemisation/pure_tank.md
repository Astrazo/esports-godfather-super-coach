# Pure Tank
- Best items: Link Line, Double-Layered Armor, Iron Tire

## Explanation
Pure tank itemisation should be aimed, as a general rule, at any hero that doesn't have any pressing mechanical reason why they should be hitting things hard. Any hero without innate effects to boost their AP or crit rate fall into this category. Heroes with effects that boost them when taking hits (like Frank, Merisi etc.) also fall in here. A purely vanilla hero with no in-fight effects falls into this category for the simple reason that if you're not doing anything special, your job is to eat the damage so the people who are doing special things get to keep doing them.

One last type of hero to throw this itemisation on are heroes who aren't vanilla, but who only need to exist in a fight to apply their effects. Examples of this include Peiniang Zhu and Big Foot - items don't really touch their effects (with some very limited examples), and they proc based on other people doing things. That means all you really want to do with them (again, aside from some specific examples) is to tank them up so they stick around to apply their effects more.

Pure tank itemisation should prioritise two stats above all else - Armor and Presence. Health is very important for tanks, certainly - but you'll usually get a good amount of that from levels and gold anyway, and a lot of tank items wind up with health on them as a matter of course. Armour, on the other hand, is a lot harder to come by and makes a huge difference in reducing incoming damage to keep your tank around a lot longer. Presence is just as vital, though, because a tank that isn't getting hit is a tank doing nothing.

Avoid pure health items like Big Guy Vest in almost all circumstances. Even on heroes with innate health scaling like Wukong and Frank, you're going to be better served grabbing armour to keep them around for longer because that health item is only going to be contributing a relatively small amount to their actual health total. Also generally avoid healing items on tanks (yes, Mandrake Elixir isn't great); they look good on paper, but in practice they're going to be eating a lot more damage than they could ever heal, such that an armour item will keep them alive and kicking for far longer. The only exception is if you have a hero with extremely high armour through other means (e.g. the Wolfgang variant that can self-stack armour).
