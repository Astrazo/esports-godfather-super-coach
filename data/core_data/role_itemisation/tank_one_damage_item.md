# Tank (one damage item)
- Best items: Self-Increasing Dumbbell, Gravity Converter, Giant Warhammer

## Explanation
These guys are very similar to Pure Tanks. The distinction between the two is that this latter category has some kind of innate mechanic to make them something of a threat - either something to boost their AP, or something to give them extra attacks.

While these heroes have something extra in their kit to make them more dangerous than a dumb brick wall, in most cases their innate mechanics aren't enough in isolation to make them any more than a dumb brick wall with a few spikes on top. For these guys you'll want to consider a standard tank build, but throwing in a single item somewhere to help them really scare the things they're hitting. Your rule of thumb should be that if you're a tank getting extra attacks (i.e. if you're Merisi) you should look for something which procs on-hit; if you're not, take something that boosts your one attack more based on your bulk.

Importantly, this category does not fit tanks that have a single-use effect. Heroes like Hakuna and Reinhardt might have some nice team-wide AoE on turn one, but after that they become vanilla (okay Reinhardt has some minor splash, sometimes, but the damage is so low as to be irrelevant). It isn't a good idea to base your item build around a single attack unless that attack is the be-all and end-all of the entire hero.

It is critical, however, to only apply this category when there's a relevant item to slot in. You can't just throw any old damage item in there and expect it to have any real relevance; a Hundred Atk Sword or Sword of Heaven isn't going to do jack. Slot in a damage item if there's an item which synergises with that hero's bonus stats or innate effects; if there isn't itemise them like a Pure Tank.

Note that Merisi, as the only real tank to get bonus attacks, is a great user of Acceleration Coil since he'll stick around long enough to stack it and his AP is low enough that pretty quickly it'll overtake his actual attacks for damage. Helium Tank is a strictly worse alternative, being more expensive and quickly being outscaled in exchange for ignoring armour. Fearless Headband, on the other hand, is pretty fantastic.

N.B. While itemisation timings vary, you'll usually want your damage item to be your second item, sometimes third. Getting an actual tank item in place is almost always necessary to let them actually function (a Merisi without a Presence item is a Merisi who never gets an extra attack). Once that's in place, THEN you can afford to get them a damage item - and you'll usually want this place second so they can start really impacting fights. Only push it to third item on heroes like Frank, for whom dealing damage is very much a luxury.
