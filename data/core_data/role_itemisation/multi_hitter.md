# Multi-Hitter
- Best items: Acceleration Coil, Helium Tank, Ghost Claw, Terror Anthology

## Explanation
These guys are a little weird. They don't necessarily hit hard, but they do hit often, and that means a whole different approach to itemisation - look for items which give you damage or stacking debuffs from attacks.

This category has a lot of flexibility in it because there are plenty of heroes that attack more than once but don't really go overboard - Elemi, for instance, along with Kid, Reinhardt and others. These heroes can be built as multi-hitters but won't excel in the role - you'll usually want to build them via one of the other categories unless they're the only multi-attacker in the match, in which case you can lean into that aspect a little more.

Multi-hitters also don't really need to be classed as such to do their job; you can build them via other categories and they'll perform just fine. This only really becomes a role in itself when there's multiple strong, appropriate items available in your version. Nonetheless, you should keep it in mind when building one of the relevant heroes. You should also bear in mind that these builds can fall flat if you're doing badly in a game for the simple reason that they rely on a lot of instances of damage; if fights are shorter and more lethal they won't have time to do their work.

## Example Builds
- Acceleration Coil, Helium Tank, Terror Anthology
- Fearless Headband, Ghost Claw, Extendable Sword
