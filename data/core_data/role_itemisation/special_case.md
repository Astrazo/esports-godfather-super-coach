# Special Case
- Best items: N/A

As with any categorisation system (at least one looking at a reasonably complex data set), there's always going to be a few outliers that just don't really fit in anywhere. Be ready to throw all these rules out the window for certain heroes, and to heavily modify them for others. I won't go into detail on these heroes - that's saved for their individual entries - but will give a brief summary of the most notable ones below:

- Peiniang Zhu: Not an AoE mage or multi-hitter but has a huge number of potential damage instances - effects which proc specifically off damage rather than attacks are amazing.
- Digo: Has absolutely no interest in defences of any kind, ever, no matter what.
- Zealot: Weirdness around clones means flat stats at the start of a fight are key.
- Babe: Massive discount on your first item. Either get something stacking very early or get that Ultimate Iron Fist for less than the price of a Little Lightning.
- Raven: Only hero that benefits from penetration more than just directly.
- Felicity: Eats debuffs. Don't use them.
