# Seat Warmer
Best items: Battle Flag, Interest Note, Herd Immunity Flyer

## Explanation
This category belongs to the heroes who sit in the Support role - and therefore get very little gold - but who have few or no in-fight mechanics. They don't really do any damage with their attacks, AP has little to no relevance to their effects. You brought them for their cards or their passive effects.

In a perfect world you'd itemise these heroes as Pure Tanks so that they would actually provide an in-fight benefit - by soaking damage - but this world is far from perfect and your support doesn't have the money to buy those kinds of nice things. Instead you should look to items which have a decent impact but cost substantially less - allowing your support to give you some relevant item spikes sometime before Hell freezes over.

Note that aside from a few extremely powerful support-buff items (Herd Immunity Flyer, most notably), if the game goes super late and/or there are version rules which promote Support gold generation, these builds will become inferior to simple Pure Tank (or, in some cases, Mage) ones. The point is that in the nine games out of ten that that DOESN'T happen, you're better off having three mediocre items than two decent ones. All the same, consider throwing cheaper tank items into this category as well to add to soak efficacy (Double-Layered Armor is a great one since it costs about the same anyway).

## Example Builds
- Interest Note, Herd Immunity Flyer, Scallion Oil Noodles
- Battle Flag, Fragile Sticker, Double-Layered Armor
