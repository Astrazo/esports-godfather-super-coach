# Mage
- Best items: Fragile Sticker, Blue Monolith, Stealth Dagger, Jar of Doom

## Explanation
At first glance, this really isn't a category. After all, it's another 'all the damage' archetype - so what's the difference between a Mage and an Assassin, or an ADC?

The answer is in what you're allowed to build. Although there's fairly similar numbers of Physical and Magical heroes, the vast majority of the latter are either mages or supports - and supports don't tend to get enough gold to qualify for this archetype.

That means that Mage itemisation is similar to your Assassins or ADCs, but with access to a set of items that far fewer heroes can use - often your Mage is the only one that has access to these items and has the gold to buy them.

Mage items also have a much greater tendency to offer big percentage damage boosts - Blue Monolith and Jar of Doom being good examples - which work out to a lot more power than the flat AP you get on physical items. They also don't have much access to crit. That means you're just looking for damage-boosts and mage-specific utility above all else, with flat AP a welcome secondary concern.
