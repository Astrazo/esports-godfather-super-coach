# Jungle Focus

## Description
Similar to Bot Lane Focus, but playing around a jungle-support pair. The ADC should be a strong general-purpose pick. This approach tends not to win as quickly since your dominant hero is not pushing a lane, but is able to spread out and help every lane at various times.

## Example Compositions
- Kid / Zealot / Dylan / Elemi / Beverly
- Qube / Kamaitachi / Bunu Shan / Digo / Beverly
